## What is not covered

- **NHTSA source documents.** The Part 573 Safety Recall Reports, and the verbatim quotes from them that appear inside the labels, run outputs and
  reports, are US-government records published by NHTSA. We do not relicense them; their status is whatever it is at the source. The PDFs themselves are
  not in this repository.
- **The MSOM dataset.** The hand-coded recall dataset of Astvansh, Ball & Josefy (2022), *Manufacturing & Service Operations Management* 24(3):1457-1473,
  doi 10.1287/msom.2022.1085, is not included in or redistributed by this repository. Obtain it from the publisher.
